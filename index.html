01 / Understanding USM

Moving beyond
paper certificates.

Proposed start1 Jan 2027
Target completion28 Jul 2027

USM enables participating securities to be held and transferred without paper certificates. Digital records replace the paper evidence of a holding, helping reduce manual administration.

This guide sets out a proposed transition for Swire Pacific shareholders. Your next steps will depend on whether you hold paper certificates in your own name or hold shares through a bank, broker or custodian.

The target date is a planning assumption for this draft, rather than a confirmed regulatory deadline or a requirement for every shareholder to convert by that date.

03 / Your next steps

Start with what you hold.

01

Identify your holding route

Do you hold paper certificates registered in your own name, or are your shares held through a bank, broker or custodian? Check each holding separately.

02

Gather your records

Locate certificates, note the registered name, share class and quantity, and keep copies for your records. Ask the registrar about missing certificates.

03

Check your registered details

Review your name, address and contact information. Joint, corporate and estate holdings may need additional authorisations and documents.

04

Wait for confirmed instructions

Use the channels linked from Swire Pacific’s official website. Follow the registrar’s identity checks and submission process, then verify the digital confirmation.

Already holding through a bank or broker? Contact your intermediary before taking action. It can explain how its arrangements apply to your holding and whether you need to do anything.

05 / Frequently asked questions

Your questions,
answered.

Draft answers for Hong Kong shareholders. Final company and registrar instructions will take precedence.

What is USM?

USM stands for Uncertificated Securities Market. It provides a framework for participating securities to be held and transferred electronically without paper certificates. See the HKEX USM information page for official background.

Is 28 July 2027 an official deadline?

No. It is the requested target completion date for this draft transition plan. The proposed programme begins on 1 January 2027. Swire Pacific’s participation date, certificate submission cut-offs and any shareholder deadlines must be confirmed separately in formal notices.

Do I need to act if my shares are held through a broker or bank?

Ask your bank, broker or custodian about its USM arrangements. Your holding is administered through that intermediary, so the steps may differ from those for shareholders holding paper certificates in their own names. Do not open a new registrar account or request a transfer solely on the basis of this draft.

How would I convert my paper certificates?

The proposed route is to check your holding details, complete any required registrar registration and identity checks, submit the certificates and supporting documents through the confirmed process, and verify the resulting digital record. Exact forms, delivery methods, service availability and processing times remain subject to the registrar’s official instructions.

Should I send in or destroy my certificates now?

No. Keep original certificates secure until the registrar provides verified instructions. Do not destroy them or send them to an address shown in an unsolicited message. This draft is an information page and cannot receive certificates or process conversions.

What if a certificate is lost or damaged?

Contact the official share registrar before attempting conversion. It will explain the evidence, replacement or other procedure needed for your circumstances, including any indemnity, notice, fee or waiting period. Keep any damaged certificate and do not assume that a photocopy is sufficient.

Will conversion change my shares, dividends or voting rights?

Changing the form in which a holding is recorded is not, by itself, intended to change the number or class of shares or the rights attached to them. Dividend payment and voting procedures depend on your holding route and the applicable notices. Check payment details and communication preferences with the registrar or intermediary rather than assuming they will be updated automatically.

Will there be a fee?

Fees have not been set for this draft. The registrar or intermediary should confirm any applicable account, conversion, transfer, replacement-certificate or other charges before you instruct it. Do not rely on this page as a promise that a service will be free.

What about joint, corporate, overseas or estate holdings?

These holdings may require additional identity evidence, signatures, corporate authorities, probate or other supporting documents. Overseas holders should also confirm accepted certification and delivery arrangements. Ask the registrar for the requirements for your specific holding before submitting documents.

Can I sell or transfer shares during the transition?

Check with the registrar and, where relevant, your broker before placing instructions while a conversion or transfer is being processed. Processing windows, document requirements and any announced cut-offs may affect when an instruction can be completed. This draft does not establish a trading suspension or guarantee uninterrupted processing.

What if I cannot use online services or miss the target date?

Ask the registrar about assisted services and the treatment of outstanding paper holdings under the confirmed company arrangements. Do not assume that missing this draft target cancels your shares, or that conversion will happen automatically. Support routes and any continuing certificate arrangements must be confirmed in official guidance.

How do I protect my personal information?

Navigate to registrar and intermediary services through verified official channels. Never disclose passwords or one-time security codes, and do not send identity documents to unsolicited contacts. This draft website has no account sign-in, certificate upload or document submission facility.
