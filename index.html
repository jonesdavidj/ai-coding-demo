The HKA experience

Big possibilities.
A personal sense of place.

Choosing a school is also choosing a community. Hong Kong Academy is a non-profit international school at 33 Wai Man Road, Sai Kung, where a shared campus brings students, educators and families together.

Discover the people, places and everyday connections that make this corner of Hong Kong special.

This is Hong Kong Academy

Real spaces.
A shared sense of belonging.

The school’s distinctive entrance and purpose-built campus place HKA firmly in its Sai Kung neighbourhood.

HKA’s facilities include spaces for the arts, science, sport and collaboration. Parent learning spaces and a café also make room for conversations and connections beyond the classroom.

Community feature

Community in action.

Real ways the HKA community connects, contributes and celebrates, as described by the school.

01 / COME TOGETHER

The Community Fair

A school gathering that brings families together and gives parent volunteers a way to contribute. HKA’s PTCO supports the fair alongside other community events.

02 / SHARE A PASSION

Choir & dragon boating

HKA lists community choir, exercise classes, rock band and dragon boating among its community activities—shared interests that open the door to new friendships.

03 / CONNECT WITH SAI KUNG

Small actions. Local impact.

Students lead beach clean-ups and connect with elderly members of the Sai Kung District Community Centre, bringing care for people and place into school life.

Our neighbourhood

More sky.
More possibility.

Sai Kung brings a different rhythm to Hong Kong life. Green hills, a lively waterfront and access to the countryside offer families space to explore, connect and recharge.

Let curiosity wander.

From coastal walks to country park trails, the surrounding landscape gives families opportunities for outdoor discovery and time together beyond the school day.
