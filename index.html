OUR CONNECTION RUNS DEEP

From the rugby pitch
to a bigger purpose.

Doddie Weir’s connection to Hong Kong began long before his MND diagnosis. When he shared his diagnosis in 2017, friends in the city rallied around him — turning friendship and a love of rugby into action.

Today, local dinners, community challenges and rugby partnerships help fund the Foundation’s global research mission. The Foundation became a registered Hong Kong charity in 2023.

THE 2025 DODDIE DINNER

27 MARCH 2025 · HOPEWELL HOTEL

A night to remember.
A reason to keep going.

More than 600 guests, including Doddie’s wife Kathy, came together for the fifth Hong Kong Doddie Dinner, raising HK$4.6 million for MND research.
